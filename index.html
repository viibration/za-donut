Ansha's Donuts

Berry & Flavor Power reference for Pokémon Legends: Z-A.

Donuts hold 3 Berries at first, up to 8 once you get better butter. Each Berry's Sweet / Spicy / Sour / Bitter / Fresh points add up to decide the donut's dominant flavor(s); Level and Calories add up to set the star rating (Calories = time in Hyperspace Lumiose, Level = the level boost while there). Berry table defaults to Hyper Berries since that's what's used post-game.

Story-locked recipes that open a portal to a Legendary encounter. Each can only be made once — the numbers below are minimum flavor totals, so hitting or exceeding all five is what matters.

As Portal Pokémon levels climb, these push Level and Calories the highest for the longest, strongest runs in Hyperspace Lumiose. Level/Calories totals are taken from the source as-is — they run higher than a plain sum of the berry stats above, likely from an undocumented quality/star-rank bonus.

A donut's dominant flavor decides which Flavor Powers it can roll. Each power has 3 levels — higher Level Budget on that flavor means better odds of a higher level.

Flavor Points → Budget Point (per flavor)

Each flavor's total points on the donut set that flavor's own budget, used to decide how many/which Flavor Powers of that flavor get rolled. A Rainbow Donut adds +1 to every flavor's budget (never exceeding 9).

Level Budget → Power Roll Odds

Once a flavor's budget is set, it rolls how many Flavor Powers of that flavor get added and at what cost (roughly: how many separate powers, and how high-level each is).

Total Flavors → Overall Budget Points

All flavor points on the donut are summed and used to set the donut's overall budget point total.
